vibator icon candidates

Three geometric marks drawn on a 32 unit grid. Each is shown at 16, 32, 64 and 256 pixels on a light and a dark background. The 256 pixel size is scaled down on narrow screens. Marks at 16 and 32 pixels are drawn at their true size.

A. Marker

a-caret.svgDefault

A lowercase v that also points down at a line, like the marker a diagnostic puts under a location. The letter carries the name; the bar carries the finding.

B. Flagged line

b-flagged-line.svg

A file drawn as three lines of text, with a mark in the gutter next to one of them. The tile keeps it legible as a favicon, but it reads as a generic document icon.

C. Selection grid

c-selection-grid.svg

The project as a grid of files, with one file picked out by a rule. It has no letterform, so it depends on the wordmark next to it.

In a browser tab

Each mark at 16 pixels next to the page title.
